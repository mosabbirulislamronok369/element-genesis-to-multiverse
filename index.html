HOW TO PLAY

  1. Each level is a mission inside the game world, not a separate quiz mode.
  2. The question bank exceeds 2000 questions and includes Bangladesh primary/high-school/college teacher preparation, Bangla grammar, ICT, general knowledge and current affairs.
  3. Choose one of four scientifically plausible answers; difficulty rises from school to college, university and honours.
  4. Three mistakes cause a 3-level penalty.
  5. Every 5 completed levels opens a scientist award.
  6. Every award shows the scientist's English + Bangla dialogue.
  7. Stone Age discoveries gradually evolve toward atoms, life, technology, quantum science, galaxies, the Multiverse and Omniverse.
  8. Level 100 correctly awards Nikola Tesla, and every completed mission fabricates an animated discovery visual.
  9. Subjects are mixed naturally: ICT, Physics, Quantum Science, Astronomy, Biology, Chemistry, Bangla Grammar, English Grammar, Bangladesh GK, Education/Pedagogy and Current Affairs.
  10. The game continues through Level 2000; the final 100-level Islamic Knowledge chamber uses English + Bangla questions and options.
  11. Evolution questions are excluded; the final sector uses Islamic knowledge concepts and bilingual English + Bangla options.

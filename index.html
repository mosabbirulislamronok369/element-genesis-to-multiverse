GAME RULES

  1. The question is a mission inside the Genesis-to-Omniverse journey.
  2. Correct answer → Fabricator → animated discovery → XP → next level.
  3. Hint gives a clue connected to the answer; it is optional.
  4. Three mistakes apply a 3-level penalty, but previously unlocked levels stay unlocked.
  5. Use LEVEL MAP anytime to replay any unlocked level.
  6. School foundations gradually rise through High School, College, University, Honours and specialist/competitive levels.
  7. Career missions include Bangladesh teacher preparation, Bangla grammar, ICT, GK and job-style questions.
  8. Anime, Marvel, TV/streaming, sports and current-affairs missions appear naturally in the journey.
  9. Evolution questions are excluded. The final Islamic/Hadith sector uses bilingual English + Bangla questions and options.

HADITH STUDY NOTE

Hadith-identification missions focus on verification, isnad, terminology and the distinction between sahih, da'if and mawdu'. The source framing follows the named work হাদীসের নামে জালিয়াতি by Dr. Khandaker Abdullah Jahangir rather than inventing unattributed hadith claims.
